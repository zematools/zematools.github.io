Font Smooth Pro

License help

Find your Font Smooth Pro license.

Check the email address you used at checkout for your license key.

Save your key somewhere private. Font Smooth verifies the license when you activate Pro.

Your license is also sent by email.

After a successful purchase, a backup license email is sent to the email address you used at checkout. Look for Dodo Payments or Font Smooth, and check Spam/Junk if it is not in your inbox.

Still missing? Email serviceamanuel@gmail.com with your checkout email and order reference. Do not send card details. You do not need to purchase again to recover a license.

Activate Font Smooth Pro

  1. Open the Font Smooth extension in your browser.
  2. Open its Pro or license section and choose Activate Pro.
  3. Paste your license key and activate. Font Smooth checks the license before unlocking Pro.

A return link alone does not activate Pro or verify a payment. Your Dodo receipt is your purchase record. Checkout currently shows the Zema Tools brand.
