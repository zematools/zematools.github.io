Font Smooth Pro

License help

Find your Font Smooth Pro license.

Check the email address you used at checkout for your license key.

Save your key somewhere private. Font Smooth verifies the license when you activate Pro.

Can’t find the license email?

  1. Open the email account you used at checkout.
  2. Search for Dodo Payments or Font Smooth. Check Spam/Junk and Promotions too.
  3. If it is in spam, mark it as not spam and keep the email for future installations.

Dodo sends your license key by email after a successful purchase.

Still missing? Don’t buy again. Email serviceamanuel@gmail.com with your checkout email and order reference from your receipt, if you have one. If you used the wrong email at checkout, mention that. Do not send card details or your full license key.

Activate Font Smooth Pro

  1. Open the Font Smooth extension in your browser.
  2. Open Settings and choose Activate a license.
  3. Paste your key in the License key field and select Activate.

Pro unlocks in the extension after your license is activated. Your license supports up to 5 active browser installations. Dodo checkout and receipts may show the Zema Tools brand.
