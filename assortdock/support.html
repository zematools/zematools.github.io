AssortDock support

Contact Zema Tools at serviceamanuel@gmail.com. Include your AssortDock version, the screen you were using and the exact message you saw. If you attach a screenshot, hide license keys and private supplier details.

Find or activate a Pro license

Look for the AssortDock or Dodo Payments email sent to the address you used at checkout. In the Windows app, open Settings → License, paste the customer license key and select Activate. The same screen shows whether Pro is active on this PC.

Move an activation

On the previous PC, open Settings → License → Deactivate on this PC. Then activate on the new PC. If the previous PC is unavailable, email support with your order reference.

Billing and refunds

Use the order details in your Dodo Payments receipt, or email Zema Tools with the checkout email address and order reference so we can locate your purchase.
