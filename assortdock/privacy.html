AssortDock privacy policy

Last updated November 27, 2026

Your catalog files

AssortDock opens and processes supplier spreadsheets on your Windows PC. Imported rows, column mappings, pricing rules and approvals are stored locally. Export creates a CSV in the location you choose. You decide whether to upload that file to Shopify or share it with anyone.

Pro purchases and activation

Dodo Payments handles checkout and issues Pro license keys. When you activate, verify or deactivate Pro, the app sends your license key and a randomly generated installation identifier to the Zema Tools licensing service hosted on Cloudflare. The service checks purchase status, license entitlement and active installations with Dodo Payments. Catalog rows and supplier prices stay on your PC during these checks.

The app protects the saved license key with Windows encryption tied to your Windows user account. You can release an activation in Settings → License → Deactivate on this PC.

Website visits and support

GitHub Pages hosts the AssortDock website. Opening a page sends technical request information, including your IP address, to the hosting provider. If you email Zema Tools, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use this information to answer your request.

Your choices and contact

You control the project copies, backups and exported files you save. For questions about personal data or to request deletion of information you have shared with Zema Tools, email serviceamanuel@gmail.com. For a purchase inquiry, include your order reference and keep your full license key private.
